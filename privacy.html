Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

DuoCaption respects your privacy. This policy explains what data DuoCaption accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored.

Data We Do NOT Collect

DuoCaption does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. We do not track your Netflix viewing history, account information, or browsing activity.

Data Stored Locally

All user data is stored locally in your browser and never leaves your device:

IndexedDB: Saved sentences and review progress are stored in the browser's IndexedDB database.

Chrome Storage (sync): Appearance settings and user preferences are stored in Chrome's sync storage.

You can delete all data at any time by removing the extension.

Host Permissions

DuoCaption requests the following host permissions for its core functionality:

netflix.com and nflxvideo.net — to display dual subtitles on Netflix video pages and access subtitle track data.

fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com — to load custom Google Fonts for subtitle display.

These permissions are used only for the stated purposes.

Third-Party Services

Google Translate: When you hover over a word in the subtitles, that word is sent to Google Translate for translation. No other data is sent.

Google Fonts: Custom fonts are loaded from Google's CDN when selected by the user in subtitle settings.

Google Analytics: The DuoCaption website (duocaption.com) uses Google Analytics to collect anonymous page view statistics. Analytics is NOT active within the Chrome extension itself.

No data is sent to any other third-party service.

Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact
